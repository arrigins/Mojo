    Implementation stages of Mojo

    Seven steps from the first call to everyday use. You fill the system stage by stage, and we stay with you the whole way.

    1. Set up a dedicated implementation chat

      Simple communication: one place for every question from your team.

    2. Kickoff call

      We present the implementation plan and align on steps and timelines.

    3. The school fills the system step by step

      After each stage, we review the data and provide recommendations.

    4. Support throughout the entire journey

      We answer all questions and help set everything up correctly.

    5. Provide training materials

      Schools train teachers and parents with ready-made guides.

    6. Intensive support1.5 months

      Ensuring the smoothest possible launch.

    7. Transition to technical support

      Through the “Support” module on the platform, we stay in touch to assist with any issues.

    Want to learn more? We’ll get in touch to discuss the details.
